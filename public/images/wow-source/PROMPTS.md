# WhizzoWow — What We Source image prompts

Save each generated image at the path shown, then the pages can be wired to them.

## Shared style line (append to every prompt)

> Premium editorial product photography, soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field, muted sage-green, cream and grey palette, clean uncluttered composition, sustainable textile brand look. No text, no logos, no people, no watermark.

## Negative prompt (if the tool has one)

> text, letters, logos, watermark, people, hands, faces, cartoon, illustration, oversaturated, cluttered background
